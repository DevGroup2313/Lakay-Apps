WHICH FILE
----------
Lakay-iPhone-iPad.ipa: one file for both. It is in the builds folder of this
repository, and on its Releases page ("Lakay for iPhone, iPad and Fire (latest
build)", under Assets).


INSTALL (OR UPDATE)
-------------------
Apple lets an app that is not from the App Store onto an iPhone or iPad only
through a computer. Tapping the file on the iPhone or iPad installs nothing.

1. On a computer (Mac or Windows), download Lakay-iPhone-iPad.ipa. In the
   builds folder, click the file's name, then the download button on the right.
2. Open Sideloadly (sideloadly.io). On Windows, Sideloadly also needs iTunes
   and iCloud from Apple's own website.
3. Connect the iPhone or iPad with its cable, and unlock it.
4. Drag Lakay-iPhone-iPad.ipa into Sideloadly, type your Apple ID, and press
   Start. Sideloadly signs the app with your Apple ID as it installs it.
5. The first time, on the iPhone or iPad: Settings, General, VPN and Device
   Management, your Apple ID, Trust. On iOS 16 or newer, Developer Mode may
   also be asked for: Settings, Privacy and Security, Developer Mode.

With a free Apple ID, the app must be installed again this way every 7 days.
The store's books stay. A paid Apple Developer account makes each install last
a year. To update, install the newer file the same way, over the old one.


THE STORE'S BOOKS
-----------------
- They are sealed on the iPhone or iPad, inside Lakay's own space. Removing
  Lakay from the device removes them, so save a backup first.
- Save a backup in Lakay at closing time: Settings, Save a backup now. The
  file is sealed and opens only with your password or the Master Key.
- Each device keeps its own books. To move a store, save a backup on one
  device and restore it on the other.
